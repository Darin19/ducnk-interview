Business	Analyst	&	QA	
Job	Description	·	Hanoi,	Vietnam	·	Full-time	
	
Job	Purpose	
• The	job	holder	is	responsible	for	executing	the	build-test-verify	cycle	of	the	company's	AI	assistant	
platform,	with	emphasis	on	iterative	configuration,	optimization,	and	quality	assurance	across	
multiple	business	domains.	
• The	job	holder	works	within	a	value	chain	of	Research	→	Requirement	→	Build	→	UAT	→	Production	
Verification,	primarily	owning	the	Build	and	Test	phases	while	supporting	Requirement	refinement.	
• The	job	holder	combines	business	analysis	skills	with	hands-on	testing	discipline,	ensuring	AI	
features	are	configured	optimally	through	repeated	iteration,	validated	thoroughly	before	release,	
and	verified	in	production	to	maintain	quality	standards.	
Key	Accountabilities	(1)	
Build	—	Document	Processing,	Configuration	&	Optimization	
• Manage	the	end-to-end	document	lifecycle:	collection,	processing,	quality	assurance,	and	ingestion	
into	the	AI	platform.	
• Execute	iterative	configuration	and	optimization	cycles	—	systematically	testing,	measuring,	and	
refining	until	quality	targets	are	met.	
• Maintain	golden	test	sets	and	track	quality	scores	across	iterations	to	ensure	measurable	
improvement	and	prevent	regression.	
• Document	configuration	decisions	and	lessons	learned	as	reusable	institutional	knowledge.	
• Collaborate	with	the	engineering	team	to	troubleshoot	issues	and	escalate	problems	that	require	
technical	intervention.	
Key	Accountabilities	(2)	
Test	UAT	&	Production	Verification	
• Design	and	execute	comprehensive	test	plans	covering	functional	flows,	edge	cases,	and	real-world	
user	scenarios.	
• Perform	end-to-end	user	acceptance	testing	with	structured	documentation	of	results,	defects,	and	
severity	classification.	
• Validate	AI	outputs	against	business	rules	and	domain	accuracy	standards.	
• Conduct	regression	testing	and	production	verification	to	ensure	no	degradation	after	changes	or	
deployments.	
• Track	and	report	quality	metrics	over	time,	flagging	trends,	regressions,	and	anomalies	for	action.
